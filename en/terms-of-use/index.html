1. Platform Role (No Healthcare Services)

Lab Online Booking is a technology platform that facilitates appointment discovery, scheduling, and check-in with independent healthcare providers.

  • All healthcare services are provided solely by independent healthcare providers

You understand that any medical care, outcomes, or decisions are the sole responsibility of the healthcare provider.

2. Not for Emergencies

The Platform must not be used for urgent or emergency medical needs.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or attend the nearest emergency department.

This Platform is designed for administrative booking purposes only.

You agree that you will not submit, upload, or enter:

  • symptoms

Lab Online Booking:

  • does not store medical records

All medical information is managed directly by the healthcare provider.
