Common Questions

Do I need a requisition in order to have laboratory testing performed?

Yes. Laboratories in British Columbia cannot perform a collection without a laboratory requisition from a health care provider. The only exception for Interior Health Laboratories is On-Demand STI Testing. Please visit Interior Health's Accessing On Demand STI Testing page (www.interiorhealth.ca/services/accessing-on-demand-sti-testing) to learn more.

How far in advance can I book an appointment?

You can book up to 3 months (90 days) in advance.

Can I walk in instead of booking?

Booking guarantees your time slot and takes under two minutes. For Fraser Health labs, walk-in hours are listed on each lab's page. For other labs, Lab Online Booking does not hold walk-in information, so contact your laboratory directly to ask.

Do I need to fast for my appointment?

Always follow the fasting instructions given by your health care provider. If you are unsure, please contact your health care provider for guidance.
