# LabOnlineBooking.ca — design preview

A clickable preview of the new LabOnlineBooking.ca design, covering every page on the live English site.

**This is not the live site.** Appointment times on the booking screens are sample data. Pages are hidden from search engines (`noindex` and `robots.txt`).

## What's here

- Every page from the live English sitemap, at the same addresses: home, About, FAQ and all 7 FAQ topics, British Columbia, the 4 health authorities and their lab lists, all 61 city pages and all 69 lab pages
- Pages the sitemap doesn't list: Contact Us, Terms of Use, Privacy, Sign In and account creation (consent, then details)
- New pages from the redesign: Find an Appointment, the Booking Assistant, a booking screen and confirmation for each lab, and For Clinics (coming soon)
- Lab names, addresses, appointment times, walk-in hours, tests and FAQ answers are copied from the live site (20 Nov 2026)

Every internal link is checked automatically before publishing. On the 20 Nov 2026 build, 298 pages had no broken links.

The production code and the booking system stay with the LabOnlineBooking.ca team. This repository is for design review only.
